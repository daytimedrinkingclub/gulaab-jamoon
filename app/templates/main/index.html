About CraftX India

CraftX India is revolutionizing the travel industry by shifting focus from destination-oriented to experience-oriented journeys. We empower stays and tourism facilitators to provide curated, personalized experiences, meeting the evolving demands of modern travelers.

Our platform helps partners stay ahead in the experience-driven market, crafting unforgettable moments and transforming the way people travel.

Our Framework

The CraftX User Journey

  • Discover:

    Users explore unique experiences tailored to their interests through our AI-powered recommendation system.

  • Plan:

    Our intelligent system helps users create personalized itineraries, considering preferences, budget, and time constraints.

  • Book:

    Seamless booking process for experiences, accommodations, and transportation, all in one place.

  • Experience:

    Travelers immerse themselves in unforgettable moments, guided by our mobile companion app.

  • Share:

    Users can easily share their experiences on social media and inspire others through our platform.

  • Reflect:

    Post-trip surveys and analytics help improve future experiences and provide valuable insights to our partners.
